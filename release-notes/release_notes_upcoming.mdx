  - Scan App

    - `/v0/events` and `/v0/events/{event_id}` responses now include the optional verdict field
      `round_number`: the oldest open mining round active at the verdict's `record_time`.
      Scan only serves it for verdicts with a `record_time` at or after the time set for the switch-over key
      `scan/always-serve-verdict-round-number` in `svOperationsSwitchOverTimes` of the DSO rules configuration.
      SVs activate it with a "Set Decentralized Synchronizer Operations (DSO) Rules Configuration" vote.
      New networks serve it from the start.
