# 10302 - globalMediatorSv2 "Locked connection was lost, trying to rebuild" (run 37634008083)

Verdict: DUPLICATE of #10438 (same WARN, same mechanism, Pawel's analysis there): the periodic read-only probe of
`DbLockedConnection` and the `DbLockPostgres: checking lock` task race for the single `KeepAliveConnection`; the probe
loses three times in a row (`NoConnectionAvailable` counts as inconclusive), `onConnectionLost` logs the WARN, and the
connection is rebuilt 80 ms later. The database was healthy. Oriol linked the 2024 instance #1283
(= canton-network-node#15508). Canton bug; the fix belongs in Canton (probe must not count "connection in use by the
lock check" as inconclusive), not an ignore pattern. Close 10302 as dup of 10438 and keep 10438 as the family issue.
