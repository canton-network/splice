suites share a shard with ScheduledPackageVettingIntegrationTest, and their order, change between runs. In passing main
run 41924692331 the suite ran in wall-clock-time (7) too, but successful jobs upload no artifact, so its suite order
could not be compared. No earlier issue names the test (search `ScheduledPackageVettingIntegrationTest`: none) in
about 140 main runs since it was added on 10-27.
