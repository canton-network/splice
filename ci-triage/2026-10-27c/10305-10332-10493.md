## 10332: `volumesnapshots ... "cometbft-migration-0-hd-pvc" not found` (deploy_basic 85574, 10-09)

Pawel already found the root cause (comment 10-12):
- The NotFound is a red herring. It comes from the timeout branch of `wait_for_pvc_backup` in
  `cluster/scripts/node-backup.sh`, which patches `$pvc_name` instead of `$backupName`.
- The backup waited on a cometBFT snapshot that can never be ready: cimain runs CantonBFT. The script fell back to
  cometBFT because `synchronizerMigration.active.sequencer.enableBftSequencer` was missing from the checked-out
  config.resolved.yaml.
- The config was missing because a concurrent deploy_basic pipeline (85584) force-pushed `build/cimain-deploy-basic`
  at 16:20:57 without the "Update config for deployment to cimain" commit. The backup job checked out that tip at
  16:21. deploy_basic has no wait_for_previous_pipeline guard.

Verdict: root-caused, not flaky. It needs an owner and two cni fixes: serialize deploy_basic per cluster (or pin
the backup checkout to its own pipeline's commit), and fix the `$pvc_name` -> `$backupName` mix-up in node-backup.sh.
Keep it open, assign it, and drop it from the inbox.
