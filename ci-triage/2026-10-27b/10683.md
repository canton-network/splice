## Root cause

A semantic merge conflict between two PRs that were each green on their own. #7976 (HTTP health probes in
docker compose, merged earlier, 66d24625eb) introduced the `httpHealthProbes` value. #7178 set
`additionalProperties: false` on every chart's values schema, and the splice-global-domain schema has no
`httpHealthProbes` property. As a result, every mediator and sequencer unittest that renders the chart is rejected.

## Fix

The fix is already on main. #8015 reverts #7178 (4b77667715). The post-merge runs after the revert are green:
40538043643 at 4b77667715 and 40541190171 at 63e197ee50.

Re-landing #7178 must add `httpHealthProbes` (and any other value added since) to the schemas.

Verdict: both issues are closable, fixed by #8015.
