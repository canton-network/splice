The reference driver inserts each batch into the shared `blocks` table with `id = max(id) + 1` under SERIALIZABLE isolation, so concurrent inserts of the four global sequencers abort each other with SQLSTATE 40001. The insert of globalSequencerSv1 lost every retry from 12:29:11 to 12:29:27.5 (backoff up to 6.373 s) while the other three kept inserting blocks without events, so a topology transaction submitted through sv1 took 13 s to be ordered and wallet onboarding ran out of retries. Earlier failures: #10197 (19.6 s stall), #10273 (38 s stall). In that shard 40001 is routine (2936 failed inserts for 9508 stored batches) and almost always resolves within 0.5 s; the failures come from the rare long chains, where the uncapped exponential backoff turns 8 lost retries into 13 s. The contention itself still has no fix or owner.
