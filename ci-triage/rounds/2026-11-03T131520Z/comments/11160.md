Flake: slow global ordering in the test setup (reference sequencer `insert block` 40001 retries) makes wallet onboarding give up; the validator app could wait for the party instead.

TokenStandardCliTestDataTimeBasedIntegrationTest (expire amulet allocations handling both locked and already unlocked amulets) on release-line-0.10.0 0ad3ce0394, simtime (2), failed in its first step, `onboardWalletUser` for alice on aliceValidator:

```
12:29:23.611 HTTP 400 Bad Request POST at '/api/validator/v0/admin/users' on 127.0.0.1:5503. Command failed, message: INVALID_PRESCRIBED_SYNCHRONIZER_ID(9,7a61b839): Not all informees are on the specified synchronizer: global-domain::122088c38f7c...::37-0, but on Set(splitwell::12205850d0f6...::36-0)
```

The new party of alice became effective on splitwell at 12:29:15.85, but on the global synchronizer only at 12:29:27.6. globalSequencerSv1 received the topology broadcast at 12:29:14.922 and ordered it only at 12:29:27.587. The reference driver orders by inserting each batch as a row into one `blocks` table shared by the four global sequencers, with `id = max(id) + 1` under SERIALIZABLE isolation, so concurrent inserts abort each other with SQLSTATE 40001 and are retried with growing backoff. From 12:29:11 the insert of globalSequencerSv1 lost every retry, while the other three kept inserting (heights 6741 to 6839, blocks without events):

```
12:29:11.392 DbStorageSingle The operation 'insert block' has failed with an exception. New kind of error: transient error (request infinite retries). Retrying after 0.05s.
12:29:21.166 DbStorageSingle The operation 'insert block' has failed with an exception. Retrying after 6.373s.
12:29:27.539 DbStorageSingle Now retrying operation 'insert block'.
12:29:27.587 SequencedSubmissionsValidator At block 6840, the submission request 624b7da8-22a0-4d4d-858b-76327b7fa692 at 1970-03-06T03:01:14.002287Z validated to: Deliver
```

Meanwhile `installWalletForUser` (ValidatorUtil.scala), which submits on the AmuletRules synchronizer, retried from 12:29:16.40 and gave up at 12:29:23.526 with `Total maximum number of retries 12 exceeded`, 4 s before the party reached global. Party allocation only waits for the participant's own Authorized topology store, not for the PartyToParticipant on the global synchronizer.

Not caused by the commit under test (it only changes a Grafana dashboard), and not a missing backport: main has no change to ValidatorUtil.scala or the test since the release line branched. The `insert block` 40001 storm is the contention of the reference sequencers sharing one Postgres in the test setup, tracked in #10139 (same storm in #10197 and #10273).

Possible fix (validator app, owner to decide): in `ValidatorUtil.onboard`, wait until the PartyToParticipant of a newly allocated party is effective on the AmuletRules synchronizer before `installWalletForUser`; this also covers slow ordering in production. No test-side fix: `onboardWalletUser` is the shared helper of every suite.

Serialization failures themselves are routine in this shard (2936 failed inserts for 9508 stored batches; 99 percent of retry chains end within 0.5 s, and no other insert lost more than 4 retries in a row), and the load at 12:29 was not unusual. Not checked: why the insert of globalSequencerSv1 lost 8 retries in a row here.
