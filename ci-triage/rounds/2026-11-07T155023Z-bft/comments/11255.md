Likely a Canton BFT issue in the sequencer onboarding window: while the global ordering topology grows from 1 to 4 sequencers, submissions are rejected, put into batches that are never ordered, or wait for an epoch that stalls until the newcomers have joined. Senders then wait for their max sequencing time. Under wall clock that costs about 60 s and usually only trips a circuit breaker or a client timeout; under simulated time it never comes, because nothing advances the sim clock during environment setup, and app initialization fails.

Here sv4 never finished joining the DSO, sv4Validator gave up waiting for `sv4_validator_user` and exited the JVM during setup of TrafficBasedRewardsSvAppTimeBasedIntegrationTest, so the shard has no test report. The topology broadcasts adding the sv4 mediator were rejected right after the switch to 4 nodes:

```

16:25:50.939 Request to send submission with id 90722ac6-... with max sequencing time 1970-01-01T00:00:20Z from PAR::sv1::12202b9dfdf2... to Set(All).
16:25:50.961 Rejecting submission request by Some(PAR::sv1::12202b9dfdf2...) with SequencerDeliverError(code = SEQUENCER_UNAVAILABLE, message = P2P connectivity is not ready (authenticated = 1 < dissemination quorum = 2), rejecting)
16:25:50.997 Send [90722ac6-...] returned an error however may still be possibly sequenced so we are ignoring the error: ...
16:27:50.740 participant=sv4Participant | Another flush cycle is currently ongoing
```

The participants treat the rejected sends as possibly in flight until 00:00:20 on the simulated clock, which stood at 00:00:01.7 when the validator gave up. Their topology outboxes stayed blocked, and the re-proposals at 16:27:50 only found the old flush cycle still open.

The duplicates show the other shapes of the same window:
- #11241 (simtime): a createValidatorRight submission of splitwellValidator rejected the same way; resubmits got SUBMISSION_ALREADY_IN_FLIGHT until the circuit breaker failed its init.
- #11242 (simtime): the send was accepted but put into a batch that sv1 disseminated once to its three not yet authenticated peers and never mentioned again; the sv2 and sv4 mediators were never added.
- #11248 (wall clock): a validator traffic top-up rejected the same way, circuit breaker WARN.
- #11257 (wall clock): at the 1 to 2 step only sv1 was authenticated, below the strong quorum of 2, so the epoch took 33 s and an `onboard/sv/sequencer` request timed out after 38 s.
- #11173 (wall clock): the first epoch after the newcomer sv4 joined stalled 23 s on its segment (two view changes); a top-up stayed in flight and tripped the circuit breaker.

Not blacklisting in any of these (canton-network/splice#8356 does not help), and not the per-transaction slowdown from canton-network/splice#8361. The wall-clock shape has been around longer (#10165).

Possible fixes:
- Canton: do not activate an ordering topology before its new members are authenticated, or resend rejected or lost sends.
- Test setup: before the other apps start, onboard the sv2-sv4 sequencers one at a time and wait until every sequencer reports all peers authenticated. With at most 3 nodes the BFT weak quorum is 1, so only the step to 4 can reject sends, and by then 3 nodes are authenticated. This removes the rejected and lost sends; it does not remove the short stall while each newcomer catches up.
