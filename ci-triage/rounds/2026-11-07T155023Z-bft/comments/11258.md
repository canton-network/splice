The ledger APIs of all four SV participants froze while sv3 and sv4 joined, because the confirmation result for one DSO transaction was never delivered: the sv4 sequencer was blacklisted during the sv3/sv4 BFT onboarding and refused the verdict of sv4's mediator. In sim time the participants never time the request out, so SvExpiredRewardsCollectionTimeBasedIntegrationTest and WalletTxLogWithRewardsCollectionTimeBasedIntegrationTest both aborted in setup when sv3 and sv4 did not finish onboarding.

No participant received a result for that request. Its decision time is 05:16:10.583Z, and the sim clock stayed below 05:15:14 for the rest of the run. The participants of sv1 to sv4 host the DSO party, so their indexers stored nothing after 17:02:57, the last event before that request. The alice, bob and splitwell participants kept indexing.

sv3's `DsoRules_SetSynchronizerNodeConfig` was committed in Canton at 17:03:17, but its completion never reached the ledger API. The call hit DEADLINE_EXCEEDED after 120 s, and every retry then failed with `UNKNOWN_CONTRACT_SYNCHRONIZERS` on the SvNodeState that the frozen DSO store still listed.

This is the blacklisting episode during sequencer onboarding described in canton-network/splice#8361. canton-network/splice#8356 (no blacklisting in tests) removes the refused send, so sv2 and sv4 would have reached the threshold.

On the Canton side, two things are open:
- the mediator does not retry a verdict send that was refused (similar to #8041 before PV 35);
- it is unclear why sv1's delayed send never went out.
