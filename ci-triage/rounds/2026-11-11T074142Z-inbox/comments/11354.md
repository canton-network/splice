Test race in LsuIntegrationTest: the second test restarts all SV and scan nodes, but aliceValidator and splitwellValidator from the first test keep running. Their triggers' BFT scan calls give up after 12 retries (about 7 s) and log a WARN when the scans take longer than that to come back.

Both tests passed; checkErrors failed on three `Consensus not reached.` WARNs from BftScanConnection of the two validators (ValidatorPackageVettingTrigger, RollForwardLsuTrigger), 7 s into the second test.

```
07:12:03.060 Stopping node sv1Scan
07:12:07.565 Task closing http binding admin service still not completed after 4500 milliseconds. Continue waiting...
07:12:10.925 sv3Scan app initialization: Initialize app finished after PT0.19746691S
07:12:11.010 The operation 'Bft call with 2 out of 3 matching responses' has failed with an exception. Total maximum number of retries 12 exceeded. Giving up.
07:12:11.010 Consensus not reached.
```

The second line is from sv1Scan; the last two are the RollForwardLsuTrigger call of splitwellValidator, the last one at WARN.

The test stops `allNodes` (scans, SVs, SV validators) "to clear any caches"; the two validators are not in that list and ran without a stop from 07:09:49 to the end of the test at 07:18:53. The first failed attempt of the call above was at 07:12:04.088, one second after the stop. The scans answered with connection refused while stopped and with 404 while initializing; the second scan was healthy again only at 07:12:10.925. That is longer than the retry budget, mostly because sv1Scan took 5 s to close its HTTP binding (the other scans had closed theirs by 07:12:03.071) and the parallel stop waits for the slowest node.

The race has been in the test since the restart was added in July; this run lost it.

Fix: in the second test, stop the two validators before the restart (`stopAllAsync(aliceValidatorBackend, splitwellValidatorBackend).futureValue` before `allNodes.par.foreach(_.stop())`), the same order the end of the test already uses; the existing `startAllSync` then restarts them.
