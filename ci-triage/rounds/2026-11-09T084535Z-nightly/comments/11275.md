In simtime, a confirmation result that misses its aggregation threshold is never delivered, and the DSO participants stop indexing for the rest of the shard. That happens when sv3's mediator is added to the group: the global mediator group then has 4 active mediators with threshold 2, and Canton's delayed verdict sender lets only 3 of them send at once. The 4th waits 3 s on the sim clock, which nobody advances.

The delay is scheduled on the sequencer client clock (`VerdictSender.scala`, `sequencerSend.clock.scheduleAfterCancelledOnShutdown(..., "delayed-send-verdict", delay)`). In simtime that clock only moves when the test advances time, and the test is waiting on the ledger. The request's decision time (00:01:00.236Z) is never reached either, so all four SV participants stop publishing from 05:32:44. sv3 init then times out in SvTimeBasedRoundMgmtIntegrationTest, and the next tests fail on the leftover environment.
